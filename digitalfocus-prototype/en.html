      PDPA and cybersecurity

      Camera systems that are safe for the people recorded and for your network

      CCTV footage is personal data under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, and internet-connected devices can be an attack route. We design for both from day one.

      Designed for PDPA compliance

      • Signs that tell people they are being recorded and why
      • Set retention periods with automatic deletion
      • Role-based viewing rights with an access log
      • Blur faces or unrelated areas when footage is shared

      Cyber-secure devices

      • Default passwords changed on every device before handover
      • Camera network separated from the office network
      • Firmware updated on schedule and whenever a vulnerability appears
      • Encrypted connections for remote viewing
